1b · Protocols

Calibration

TESTED FTP is 249 W (29 Sep 2026, indoors on the KICKR in resistance mode, paced off the 4iiii: best 20 min 261.7 W x 0.95). PLANNING stays 250 W, since 1 W is inside the meter's noise, and the 255 W stretch reading is not supported. The IF cap uses the tested 249 W, which is 189 W today. The meter is self-consistent -- ERG blocks read ~200 W against a 200 W target -- but that only shows ERG control works: its absolute accuracy is untested, and it may be left-only (data TODO). The test series is INDOORS from here, on this same setup (resistance or passive mode, never ERG, same warm-up), because an indoor number reads differently from an outdoor one and mixing them breaks the trend. One OUTDOOR 20-min TT each spring (weeks 27 and 81, same road segment) cross-checks the series and is kept out of the indoor trend. No ramp tests. IRONMAN race watts = the lower of IF 0.76 x the latest tested FTP and the highest power that has passed the durability gate (outdoor 4 h ride, 3x40' at that power, power fade <5% and Pw:HR drift <5%). Never buy a bike split with intensity: it trades ~5 bike minutes for ~15 run minutes. The intervals.icu FTP setting (280 W) is ~10% high: set it to 249 W and run threshold to 3:35/km so platform TSS, IF and CTL are honest.

Aero

Every CdA here is EFFECTIVE CdA in 4iiii watts from one model (`trinity aero`): station wind at 10 m scaled x0.65 to rider height; headwind per 1 km segment from the course's actual bearing (v cos theta); measured course length; and the SAME air density, rolling resistance and drivetrain loss for the fit and the prediction (rho 1.20, Crr 0.0045, eta 0.975, 83 kg system). Race-time fits: Malbork 2025 0.245 and Mietków 2026 0.252 (no aero); Sieraków 2026 0.237, Syców 2026 0.239 (meter failed; at an assumed 225 W) and Wolsztyn 2026 0.220 (disc). The earlier 0.158-0.210 fits were biased low: that model charged all climbing as lost energy, split every course half into the wind, and dropped ~4% of Syców's distance. Disc plus deep front is worth 0.010-0.020 against training wheels; add 0.003-0.008 over 4.5 h for position decay and sitting up to drink. PLANNING: CdA 0.232 Malbork, CdA 0.225 Hamburg, CdA 0.226 for the 70.3s. The stretch targets need CdA 0.194 (8:50, at 201 W) and CdA 0.185 (8:40, at 209 W) -- 0.03-0.04 below every disc fit so far, which is a position change, not equipment. The athlete's own 4:45 current-fitness bike implies CdA 0.198, so 8:50 and his self-assessment rest on the same unmeasured number. FIRST AERO TEST in weeks 4-10, first calm day: Chung virtual-elevation on a closed loop, >=6 laps at two speeds, race kit and race bottles. Then every spring and autumn.

Fueling

Wolsztyn 2026: GI distress from km 3 of the run after gels on the bike; the gut 'unlocked' around km 18, i.e. ~70 minutes of little absorption. Actual Wolsztyn intake is not recorded yet (data TODO); if it was <=60 g/h, this plan RAISES gut load, deliberately and progressively: 60 g/h (autumn) -> 75 (winter) -> 85 (spring) -> 85-95 g/h on the bike in the IM blocks, 60-75 g/h on brick runs. Ceiling 95 g/h. CARBOHYDRATE AND FLUID ARE SET SEPARATELY: one concentrated 1:0.8 glucose:fructose bottle (or hydrogel) plus plain water bottles, so g/h does not depend on ml/h. Gels on the run are fine and stay. FLUID = 60-80% of measured sweat rate for the conditions, and never finish a session heavier than you started; until two sweat tests are done, 500-800 ml/h and drink to thirst above that. Sodium 600-800 mg/L. LONG-GUT VALIDATION before Malbork: one 5 h outdoor ride at 85-95 g/h in race-morning conditions in weeks 46-48, GI-scored -- Syców's 2:15 bike does not validate 4:40 of intake. Last 30' of every race-sim ride is fluid only, then fuel the brick. No new product inside 6 weeks of an A race. FALLBACK LADDER (GI score >=3 at Syców, the weeks 46-48 ride or the tune-up drops one rung for good): (1) 75 g/h, one CHO bottle plus water; (2) first 2 h semi-solid, liquid after; (3) hydrogel for the last hour only; (4) a 60 g/h floor with full fluid and sodium. IN-RACE RESCUE IS MANDATORY: at the first sign of the Wolsztyn pattern stop carbohydrate for 10', water or cola only, walk one aid station, resume at 30 g/h and build back. At IM distance the same failure after 4:40 of bike intake leaves 25+ km to run in deficit. Caffeine <=3 mg/kg, first dose >=90' into the bike, never from a new source.

Durability

Measured by `trinity durability` from raw streams: Pa:Hr for runs, Pw:Hr for rides, plus pace/power FADE, which catches the failure decoupling misses (output and HR falling together). The fuel-failure signature fires on 6 sessions, including Wolsztyn's run. Power fade cannot be measured on ERG, where power is held by construction, so the 32 indoor rides report decoupling only and every fade target is ridden OUTDOORS (or indoors in level/resistance mode). If the 4iiii is left-only, part of any power fade -- including Malbork 2025's 12.2% (176 -> 154 W) -- may be L/R balance shifting with fatigue. TARGETS: bike power fade <5% and Pw:HR drift <5% on the 4 h 3x40' gate ride (weeks 46-48, 84-86); run pace fade <3% on the long IM-pace brick.
